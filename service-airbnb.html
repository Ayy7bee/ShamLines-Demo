Frequently asked questions

Do I need to be in Damascus?

No. We handle everything on site, so you can own the property from anywhere and still follow your bookings.

Who talks to the guests?

We do. We answer questions, confirm bookings, welcome guests and solve any problem during their stay.

Can I still use the property myself?

Yes. Tell us which dates you need and we will block them in the calendar.
