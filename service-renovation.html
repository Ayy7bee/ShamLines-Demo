Frequently asked questions

Can you work on an apartment I am renting out?

Yes. We can plan the work around your tenants or carry it out while the property is empty, so you lose as little rent as possible.

Do you give a price before starting?

Yes. After visiting the property we give you a clear quote that explains what is included, and we agree it with you before any work begins.

Can you renovate a property I want to sell?

Yes. We focus on the improvements that add the most value, so the property looks its best to buyers.
