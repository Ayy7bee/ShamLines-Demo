Add a property

Fill in the form, add photos, then copy the line and download the photos. This page does not publish anything by itself.

Property details. Anything you leave empty is simply not shown on the property's page.

Map (optional). Paste a Google Maps link, GPS coordinates (like 33.5138, 36.2765) or just an address. In Google Maps you can right-click the exact spot and click the numbers at the top of the menu to copy its coordinates. Short links that start with maps.app.goo.gl cannot be read, so open them first and copy the long link from the address bar.
